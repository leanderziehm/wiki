## Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

Compute Engine	
1 non-preemptible `e2-micro` VM instance per month in one of the following US regions:
Oregon: `us-west1.`
Iowa: `us-central1.`
South Carolina: `us-east1.`
`30 GB disk` -months standard persistent disk.
`1 GB internet `of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations (excluding China and Australia) per month.
Your Free Tier e2-micro instance limit is by time, not by instance. Each month, eligible use of all of your e2-micro instances.

• Instance Type: Limited to 1 `e2-micro` instance per month.
• Eligible Regions:
	• us-west1 (Oregon)
	• us-east1 (South Carolina)
	• us-central1 (Iowa)
• Storage: Up to 30 GB of Standard persistent disk storage per month. Warning: Balanced, SSD, or Extreme persistent disks are not free.
• Network Egress: Up to 1 GB of network egress per month from North America to all general region destinations (excluding specific high-cost regions like China and Australia).
• Network Tier: You must use the Standard network tier. Using the Premium tier for egress can trigger charges


source:
https://docs.cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features#compute
